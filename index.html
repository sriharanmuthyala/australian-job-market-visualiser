Where Australia Works

Australia’s workforce, one occupation at a time Built by Sri Muthyala & Natasha

Explore how Australia’s workforce is distributed today and how occupations are projected to change through 2035. Each rectangle is an ANZSCO occupation, sized by employment. Use the controls to compare official Jobs and Skills Australia / ABS measures — employment outlook, skill level and median weekly earnings — with an experimental AI task-exposure estimate. The AI layer is not a forecast of job loss: it estimates how much the task mix of an occupation may be affected by current and near-term AI, while actual employment also depends on demand, regulation, human interaction, physical work and other factors. Click a tile for its JSA profile, or a group heading to zoom in.
